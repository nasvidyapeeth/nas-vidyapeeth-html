Payment Instructions

Please read before submitting any fee.

  1. Confirm your Student ID, name, class and selected months with the school office.
  2. Check the approved fee amount and any applicable discount or late fine.
  3. For hand cash, pay at the authorized office and collect a verified receipt.
  4. For UPI or bank transfer, use only payment details officially provided by the institution. Keep the transaction reference.
  5. A transfer is not confirmed until the institution verifies it. Do not treat a draft receipt as proof of payment.
  6. Keep your final receipt for future reference and contact the office if any detail is incorrect.
